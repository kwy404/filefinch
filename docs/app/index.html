How does this work without a server?

The invite holds what the other browser needs to find this one: network addresses and a key fingerprint, compressed into the link. The reply carries the same from the other side. With both, the two browsers connect directly with WebRTC.

Public STUN servers only tell each browser its own public address. They never see your files. If both devices sit behind strict firewalls, a direct path may be impossible; then a TURN relay you add in Settings forwards the encrypted packets without being able to read them.

Sync a folder between two devices

Pick a folder here and the matching folder on your other device. filefinch compares both lists (path, size, date and SHA-256) and moves only what changed. Edits made on both sides become conflicts you decide on.

Folder sync needs a browser that can write to a folder you pick, such as Chrome or Edge on a computer. You can still send whole folders from the Send tab.
